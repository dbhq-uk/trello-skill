# Tesco (UK) store preset

Canonical aisle order for a typical UK Tesco superstore, entrance to checkout. Use this order to classify and position cards. Position gaps of 1000+ between sections, ~100 within a section.

## Aisle order

1. **🍓 Fruit** (store entrance)
   - Bananas → apples/pears → mango → berries → grapes → melon/pineapple → peaches. (Eating fruit only; avocado and cooking citrus go in Veg.)
2. **🥬 Veg**
   - Root veg (incl. mash and prepared potatoes) → onions/garlic/ginger → cooking citrus (lemons/limes) → tomatoes → peppers/chillies → salad/leaves (cucumber, lettuce, spinach, radishes, avocado) → green veg (broccoli, green beans, cabbage) → fresh herbs → mushrooms
3. **🍖 Meat & Fish**
   - Chicken/turkey/poultry → pork → beef/lamb/mince → bacon/sausages/chorizo → fish (salmon, smoked salmon, prawns)
4. **🥡 Chilled Meals & Deli**
   - Chilled ready meals → fresh filled pasta/gnocchi → fresh chilled sauces → deli (nduja, salt beef, charcuterie, dips/hummus, olives)
5. **🧀 Cold Section (chilled dairy)**
   - Cheese (incl. burrata, feta, mozzarella, halloumi) → milk/cream/oat milk → yoghurt. (Butter and eggs are **not** here - see sections 11 and 12.)
6. **🧃 Chilled Juice**
   - Orange/apple juice and chilled smoothies, in the fridge run at the end of the dairy aisle. Ambient squash and fizzy drinks are section 17.
7. **🥫 Tins & Jars**
   - Passata and tinned tomatoes → tinned beans/pulses → tinned fish → tinned fruit → tinned veg
8. **🥫 Sauces & Pickles**
   - Jarred cooking sauces → table sauces/condiments (mayonnaise, ketchup, chipotle) → pickles (gherkins, cornichons, jarred jalapeños) → honey/syrups/glazes/jams
9. **🥣 Baking**
   - Flour (incl. pizza/00 flour) → sugar → baking ingredients → cocoa
10. **🫓 Bread & Bakery**
    - Loaves → flatbreads/pittas → wraps → bagels → shortbread/teacakes. (Crackers are snacks - section 16.)
11. **🥚 Eggs**
12. **🌶️ Spices, Seasonings & Cooking Ingredients**
    - Tomato purée → dried herbs → ground spices/paprika/chilli → salt/pepper → recipe seasoning mixes (fajita, taco, cottage pie, gravy, cheese powder) → butter and cooking fats → oils/vinegars (olive, vegetable, balsamic). Asian oils and vinegars - sesame oil, rice wine vinegar - are shelved in world foods, section 13.
13. **🍝 Pasta, Rice, Noodles & World Foods**
    - Pasta → rice/grains → garlic paste/purée → noodles → soy sauce, sesame oil, rice wine vinegar and other world-food sauces → world foods (curry pastes, miso, coconut milk)
14. **🥣 Cereal & Breakfast**
    - Cereal → porridge/oats → breakfast bars → spreads (peanut butter, jam if not shelved with the honey)
15. **🍵 Hot Drinks**
    - Tea → coffee
16. **🍪 Snacks**
    - Crisps/savoury snacks → crackers → biscuits → nuts/seeds → chocolate
17. **💧 Soft Drinks** (ambient)
    - Water/soda → squash → fizzy. Chilled juice is section 6.
18. **🧊 Frozen** (group with drinks if standalone, e.g. just "ice")
19. **🥂 Beer, Wine & Spirits**
    - Beer → sparkling wine (prosecco, crémant, champagne) → white wine → rosé → red wine → fortified → spirits → liqueurs
20. **🧴 Health & Beauty**
    - Skincare/facial care → toiletries → cotton pads → toothpaste → soap → toilet roll
21. **🧽 Household / Cleaning**
    - Laundry (detergent, sachets, softener) → dishwasher tabs → cleaning sprays → batteries → bin bags
22. **🐾 Pet** (if present)

## Within-section sub-ordering (the small touches)

- Produce: keep tomatoes together, keep herbs together, mash and prepared potatoes next to the loose potatoes
- Chilled: cheese → milk/oat milk → yoghurt, then the juice fridge at the end of the run
- Tins: passata and tinned tomatoes first, then beans, then tinned fruit
- Sauces: cooking sauces before table sauces, pickles last
- Bakery: savoury bread first, sweet bakery last
- Seasonings: loose spices before recipe mixes, then butter and oils
- World foods: dry goods (pasta, rice, noodles) before sauces (soy, curry paste, coconut milk)
- Drinks: hot → soft → frozen → sparkling → white → red → spirits → liqueurs
- Existing items keep their relative order within a section unless it improves the flow

## Common gotchas

- **Butter and eggs are ambient at Tesco, not in the cold section** - eggs sit just after the bread aisle (section 11), butter with the cooking fats and oils (section 12). The emoji reference groups them with dairy; that is an emoji map, not an aisle map.
- **Chilled juice is not a soft drink.** Orange juice and chilled smoothies are in the dairy fridge run (section 6); squash and fizzy are section 17.
- **Crackers are snacks, not bakery** - they sit with the crisps at Tesco.
- "Crement" / "Crément" → Crémant (sparkling wine); a bare quantity with no bullet is usually a missed bullet - assume `1 ×`
- "Pinot Noi" → Pinot Noir
- Items the user calls "cupboard staples" go in the ambient sections (7, 8, 12, 13) regardless of which aisle Tesco actually shelves them in
- Sesame oil, rice wine vinegar, Shaoxing rice wine and crispy chilli oil are world foods (section 13), not the oils shelf in section 12
- Recipe seasoning mixes (fajita, taco, cottage pie, gravy, hard cheese powder) group together at the end of the spice run, not scattered through the ambient aisles
- Caster sugar can appear in the dairy section of recipes (it is not dairy) - file under baking
- Shortbread typically lives in the bakery aisle at Tesco, not the biscuit aisle
- Fresh garlic is Veg; garlic paste/purée is world foods; jarred jalapeños are pickles
