# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which applies here in full.

The short version: everyone is welcome. Be kind, be patient, and assume the
other person meant well. Do not harass, insult or belittle anyone.

An issue about a board is usually an issue about somebody's work in progress.
Redact card titles and member names before you paste a payload, and assume a
board that looks disorganised had a reason.

## Reporting

If someone behaves in a way that breaks this, or something here makes you
uncomfortable, email <dan@dbhq.uk>. Reports are handled privately, and you will
get a first response within 48 hours.
